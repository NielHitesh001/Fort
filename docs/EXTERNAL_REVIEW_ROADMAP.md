# External review roadmap

Proposed 2026-09-14; no commissioned reviewer, approved budget or committed delivery
date is evidenced by this remediation. The founder owns scope, budget and scheduling.

1. Security: commission independent review of network parsers, arithmetic, memory
   ownership, concurrency and audit integrity against a written threat model.
   Deliver reproducible findings, regression tests and a retest report. Unresolved
   critical/high findings block deployment of the affected scope.
2. Regulatory scope: qualified counsel and domain specialists review the intended
   activity, jurisdictions, licenses, data rights, custody/reporting responsibilities
   and each proposed regulatory model. Deliver an applicability matrix, identified
   gaps and named responsible owners. Model tests are not legal validation.
3. Operations: independently exercise the target deployment with representative
   hardware, network faults, persistence failures, recovery, observability and
   access controls. Agree recovery objectives before measuring them; record actual
   results instead of assigning an unsupported universal MTTR.

For each workstream obtain comparable written proposals identifying deliverables,
reviewer independence, price, assumptions, retest cost, data handling and permitted
publication. Budget and schedule remain **TBD pending quotes and owner approval**.
No vendor engagement or external message is authorized by this document itself.

Reviews may overlap after scope is stable. Release gates require accepted evidence,
resolved findings and explicit accountable-owner approval for a particular system
and deployment. Completion of three reviews does not automatically authorize a
regulated business or justify an unqualified production/compliance claim.

Current outreach narrative: Fort is a research trading simulator for market
microstructure experiments, local benchmarks and engineering prototypes. No
verified customer traction, financing, production SLA or regulatory certification
is established here. Private decks and sent emails were not available for review;
the founder should apply this scope to those materials before reuse.
