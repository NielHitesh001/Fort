# Customer Discovery Playbook

## Goal

Secure one warm intro and one discovery call with a prospect in the target list within the first 4 days.

## Targets

- JPMorgan
- BofA
- HSBC
- Citi
- Goldman
- Nestlé
- Toyota
- Unilever
- Wise
- Remitly

## Warm Intro Strategy

Prioritize the lowest-friction paths:

1. Wise / Remitly
   - fintech, payments, treasury, international settlement concerns
   - likely easiest access to operational leaders
2. Nestlé / Toyota / Unilever
   - treasury and FX / working-capital workflow
3. Investment banks
   - trading tech, market-data infrastructure, or treasury operations groups

## Warm Intro Email Template

Subject: Quick intro to [Target CTO / Treasury Head]?

Hi [Network Contact],

I'm exploring a real-time ITCH market-data and execution-observability prototype for institutional trading teams. It combines simulated market-data reconstruction, local tamper-evident audit primitives, and configurable strategy logic for discovery conversations.

[Target Company] seems like a natural fit because [specific reason: treasury optimization, FX workflow, settlement latency, or compliance visibility].

Would you be open to introducing me to the right person there—ideally a trading-tech lead, treasury head, or operations leader? I'd be very grateful for a quick intro.

I'd love to show them the architecture and discuss whether a 2-week pilot makes sense.

Thanks,
Niel

## Discovery Script (30 minutes)

### Question 1
"Walk me through your current FX / settlement / execution workflow."

Listen for:
- manual latency bottlenecks
- retry loops
- process handoffs
- compliance or audit overhead

### Question 2
"What does a 2ms latency miss cost you per month?"

Ground the problem in operational value.

### Question 3
"If you could see the live ITCH feed plus a reliable audit trail in real time, what would you do differently?"

Listen for:
- strategy execution
- confirmation checks
- reconciliation work
- internal compliance review or analyst tooling

### Question 4
"Would a 2-week pilot make sense to validate this?"

If they say yes, propose a pilot with:
- SLA definition
- DPA/SEA structure
- daily reporting
- review after 2 weeks

## After the Call

- Send a thank-you email within 2 hours
- Attach the pilot overview and DPA/SEA draft
- Ask for a follow-up call if they are interested
- Document the decision-maker and timeline

## Success Criteria

- 1 warm intro scheduled by Day 4
- 1 discovery call completed by Day 5
- 1 serious follow-up opportunity by Day 7
